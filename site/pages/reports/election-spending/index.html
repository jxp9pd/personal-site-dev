What is counted—and what remains unknown

Broad reported federal election activity

OpenSecrets includes candidate campaigns, party election activity, 527 groups’ federal spending, reported outside spending, PAC overhead, and conventions, subject to its historical methodology changes. It removes major candidate and party transfers to avoid double counting.

Disclosed spending by a “dark money” organization can be counted even when its donors are unknown. Super PAC fundraising that remains unspent is not added to expenditures.

A public-data estimate with coverage limits

State and local races, ballot measures, lobbying, and unreported political activity are outside this series. Many issue ads do not have to be reported to the FEC. These charts therefore cannot represent every dollar spent in politics.

The source’s Democratic, Republican, and third-party amounts do not always add to the national total. The residual is preserved below rather than assigned to either major party.

Methodology, source links, and downloads
  • Party totals: current OpenSecrets overview, cross-checked against the exact-dollar historical table and export. Party attribution is OpenSecrets’ published attribution, not a reconstruction from donor identities.
  • Outside categories: OpenSecrets historical table excluding party committees. Independent expenditures explicitly advocate election or defeat without coordination. Electioneering communications cover certain candidate mentions in broadcasts near an election. Communication costs cover reportable candidate advocacy directed to restricted audiences such as members or employees.
  • Inflation: Minneapolis Fed / BLS annual CPI-U, using the displayed one-decimal values. Reported dollars × 313.7 ÷ the ending year’s CPI = approximate 2024 dollars. One ending-year CPI is applied to the full two-year cycle; this is not transaction-level deflation.
  • Comparability: detailed cost methodology changes from 2010; PAC overhead from 2014; JFC overhead from 2022. Pre-2010 outside spending is not zero: Super PACs began in 2010, but other groups existed earlier.
  • Snapshot status: retrieved October 19, 2026. OpenSecrets does not state a clear filing cutoff for this historical cost snapshot. These are its currently published historical estimates, not a guarantee of final or exhaustive 2024 spending.
