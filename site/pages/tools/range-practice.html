    How the distances are picked

    Weighted using the approximate Shot Scope frequencies for a 21–25 handicap, excluding tee shots and anything under 50 yards. Each shot is an independent draw; repeats are possible.

    Yardages are evenly sampled within each bucket. We include 50 in the 51–75 bucket and spread the open-ended 201+ bucket over 201–250 yards.
